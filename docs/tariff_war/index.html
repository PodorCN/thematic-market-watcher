USTR's 60-Economy Section 301 Tariffs (10–12.5%) Take Effect — Replaces Struck-Down IEEPA Regime

The July 28 Federal Register notice rebuilds the tariff wall struck down 6-3 in March (Learning Resources v. Trump) on the sturdier Section 301 foundation, imposing 10% or 12.5% (net of MFN) on 60 partners. It sets the legal floor for every subsequent stack — Canada 50% Section 338, China 70% chips — and creates the TRQ carve-outs that will decide who keeps margin.
